# Medical disclaimer and intended use

**Intended users.** Registered medical practitioners and other qualified healthcare professionals.

**What the app does.** It summarises published guidelines and official drug labels and links to each source,
so a clinician can find and check them quickly. Every recommendation is attributed to the body that made it.

**What the app does not do.** It does not diagnose, does not choose treatment for a patient, and does not
replace clinical judgement, local protocols or the full text of the cited source. Calculators reproduce
published formulas and thresholds; their output must be interpreted by the clinician.

**Verify before acting.** Guidelines change and summaries can contain errors. Check doses and
recommendations against the linked source and the individual patient — including renal and hepatic
function, allergies, interactions, pregnancy and breastfeeding status, and locally available formulations.

**No patient data.** The app has no network access and stores no patient information. Values entered into
the calculators are kept in memory only while the screen is open.

and the authors accept no liability for any use of them (see LICENSE and CONTENT-LICENSE.md).
